# A-Freeze-in-Time---Snowglobe

A Freeze in Time is a winter-themed web photo booth built for Hack Club's Snowglobe event by Sophel and Alicia. When we found out the theme was Freeze, we started brainstorming everything that could be frozen and thought of the idea of freezing a moment in time through photos. The site lets you take live pictures with your webcam or upload photos from your device, choose a classic print or illustrated frame, add a custom caption, and save your finished print.
